## Daily Practices (5–10 Minutes)

| Practice | How to Do It |
|---|---|
| **Morning meditation** | Listen to audio recordings from the retreat before checking your phone |
| **Journal entry** | One sentence: “What do I notice about myself today?” |

Additional journal prompts:
- “What am I feeling right now—in my body and in my thoughts?”
- “What felt true to me today—not someone else’s expectation, but my own choice?” |

## Weekly Practices (30–45 Minutes)

| Practice | Description |
|---|---|
| **A page in your workbook** | The “Integration” section—no rules, just write whatever comes to mind |
| **Silent walk** | 30–40 minutes without your phone, music or podcasts |

## What to Expect in the First 30 Days

| Period | What You May Experience | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | Euphoria or a sense of emptiness—both are normal | Give yourself a gentle transition; do not rush back into your routine |
| Days 7–14 | Daily life takes over, and insights begin to fade | Open your journal and reread your retreat notes |
| Days 14–30 | Early changes or resistance | Do not force anything—notice and write things down |
| After 30 days | A new sense of normal | Reflect: what has actually changed? |

## Checklist for the First 30 Days

- [ ] Wrote down my main insight from the retreat
- [ ] Chose 1–2 daily practices
- [ ] Set aside time for weekly reflection
- [ ] Told people close to me that something has changed (optional)
- [ ] Contacted other group participants at least once

## As Needed

- Ask for support in the participants’ chat
- Have an individual session with a psychologist (30 minutes for “Rhythm of Harmony”, 60 minutes for “Your Own Summit”)

## Supporting Yourself in the Long Term

- A monthly “check-in with yourself”: 1 hour with no tasks, just your journal and the question “How am I?”
- Listen to meditations from the retreat when you feel unsure of your direction
- Return to your workbook when making major decisions

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When will the coordinator contact me after the retreat?**
The coordinator will contact you first, 2 days after the retreat ends (20 June 2026), and suggest writing down your insights and choosing practices.

**Do I need to do all the practices?**
No. Choose 1–2 daily practices that feel right for you and keep doing them. It is better to do less, but regularly.

**How much time do the practices take?**
The minimum is 5 minutes a day (meditation or journaling). This is enough to stay connected with yourself.

**Can I contact the coordinator if things become difficult?**
Yes, at any time. For acute distress, contact the organiser, Tanya: @tatidzufri.
