## Where the Retreat Takes Place
The retreat takes place in the Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy. You will receive the exact accommodation address after your booking is confirmed.

## How to Get There

### Flights
The nearest airport is **Innsbruck (INN)**. You book your own return flights to Innsbruck.

Recommendations:
- Choose a flight that arrives in time for the **12:00** meeting on the first day of the programme
- Book your return flight for the evening of the last day or the following day
- Allow extra time: the journey from the airport takes 1.5–2 hours on mountain roads

### Airport Transfer
The transfer is **included** in the “Rhythm of Harmony” and “Your Own Summit” packages.

- Meeting point: Innsbruck Airport, arrivals hall, **Exit A**
- Departure time: **12:00** on the first day of the programme
- Driver’s contact details (Marco): +39 000 000 0000 — shared 2 days before arrival

**If your flight arrives much earlier than 12:00:** ask the organiser whether an earlier meeting or a separate transfer with reimbursement is possible.

### Arriving by Car
Let the organiser know in advance. You will receive the exact coordinates of the accommodation.

## Return Transfer
The return airport transfer is included in packages with in-person participation. The departure time is agreed with the group during the retreat.

## Phone Reception and Internet
- Mobile reception in the mountains may be unreliable
- Wi-Fi is available at the accommodation, but we recommend a “digital detox” during the programme
- In an emergency, you can always contact people outside the retreat

## Frequently Asked Questions About Logistics

**Which city is the airport in?**
The airport is Innsbruck (INN), where INN is the airport code. It is the nearest major airport to the accommodation.

**Is the transfer included in the price?**
Yes, the transfer between the airport and the accommodation is included in the “Rhythm of Harmony” and “Your Own Summit” packages. The “Path to Self” package is online only, so no transfer is needed.

**Where exactly is the accommodation?**
You will receive the exact address after your booking is confirmed. This helps protect your safety and privacy.

**Can I get there independently, without going through Innsbruck Airport?**
Yes, you can arrive in your own car. Let the organiser know in advance, and she will send you the coordinates.

**What should I do if my flight is delayed?**
Let the organiser (@tatidzufri) know immediately so she can coordinate your transfer.
