## Programme Philosophy
The programme is not planned minute by minute. It creates a rhythm rather than a schedule. Each day has its own theme. We gradually move deeper: the first days focus on settling in and opening up, while the final days focus on integration and direction.

## General Structure of the 5 Days

### Day 1: Arrival and Settling In
- Check-in and getting to know the space
- Evening group introduction circle
- Agreeing on guidelines for a safe space
- Gentle meditation to help you settle in

**Theme:** “I am here. I allow myself to pause.”

### Day 2: Connecting with Yourself
- Morning practice (meditation, breathing)
- Art session: “Who am I now?” — an image of your current state
- Group session: exploring your main roles in life
- Silent walk (individual)

**Theme:** “Who am I beyond my roles?”

### Day 3: Going Deeper
- Morning practice
- Individual art session: working with the main question you want to explore
- Group psychological session (existential analysis)
- Free time for quiet and rest

**Theme:** “What is holding me back? What is calling me?”

### Day 4: Transformation and Inner Resources
- Morning practice
- Art session: “An Image of What I Want”
- Group session: values and meaning
- Evening walk + campfire (weather permitting)

**Theme:** “What do I truly want?”

### Day 5: Integration and Farewell
- Morning practice
- Final artwork: “Road Map”
- Closing circle: insights and intentions
- Farewell breakfast
- Check-out by 12:00

**Theme:** “I know my next step.”

## What Makes the Programme Unique
1. **Small group.** Up to 10 people — space for everyone
2. **Connection with nature.** Walks and outdoor practices are part of the therapy
3. **A combination of methods.** Art therapy + existential analysis + meditation work together
4. **No “right” result.** Everyone finds their own unique answer
5. **Gentle pace.** There is time for quiet, rest and processing your experiences

## Participation Is Optional
The main principle is **being gentle with yourself**. If you want to skip a practice, you have every right to do so. There is no pressure.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Programme

**How many days does the retreat last?**
5 days: 14–18 June 2026 (the current retreat). Day 1 is arrival, and Day 5 is departure.

**Is there free time?**
Yes. Each day includes at least 2 hours of personal time to use as you wish.

**Can I skip a practice?**
Yes. There is no pressure. This is your time and your rhythm.

**Is the programme the same for every retreat?**
The basic structure stays the same, but each retreat has its own rhythm. The facilitator adapts the programme to the group.
