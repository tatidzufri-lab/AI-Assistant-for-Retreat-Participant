## Why Prepare?
A retreat is more than a trip. It is inner work that begins long before you reach the mountains. The more mindfully you approach the start, the deeper and more valuable your experience can be.

## Online Course (Before the Retreat)
Participants in the “Rhythm of Harmony” and “Your Own Summit” packages receive access to the online course as soon as they pay the deposit.

**What the online course includes:**
- Video modules on self-discovery (4 topics)
- Written practices (journaling exercises)
- Audio meditations for daily practice
- A support chat with a facilitator

**Recommended pace:** 1 module per week. It is best to start the course 3–4 weeks before the retreat.

**When access opens:** as soon as you pay the 30% deposit.

## Setting an Intention
3 days before the retreat (11 June 2026), the assistant facilitator will ask a few questions for reflection:

- “What brought you to this retreat at this point in your life?”
- “What would you like to explore or understand about yourself?”
- “What are you ready to let go of, or bring with you as an intention?”

There is no need to find the “right” words. Your answers are a personal document for you alone. They will help you engage more deeply with the programme.

## Practical Preparation (2 Weeks Before the Start)
- Tell the organiser about any allergies and dietary requirements (@tatidzufri)
- Let colleagues and family know that you will have limited contact (recommended)
- Finish urgent tasks so you can disconnect without worrying about them
- Pack according to the packing list (file 10_packing_list.md)

## Psychological Preparation

### What Is Normal Before the Retreat:
- Nervousness and mild anxiety — these can be signs that you are approaching something important to you
- Doubts such as “What if I do not fit in?” or “What if it does not work for me?” — these are common and understandable
- Expectations — it is natural to have them, but try to set them aside and arrive with an open mind

### How to Prepare Yourself:
- Give yourself permission to slow down a little in the days before you leave
- Limit news and social media for 1–2 days before you leave
- Get a good night’s sleep the night before

## Frequently Asked Questions About Preparation

**Do I need to read anything specific before the retreat?**
No. No special preparation is required. All you need to bring is yourself.

**What if I do not finish the online course?**
That is fine. The retreat is a complete experience on its own. The course helps, but you do not need to finish it to have a deep experience.

**When does the online course become available?**
As soon as you pay the 30% deposit. The organiser will send you the access link.

**Can I bring something to read?**
Yes, if reading helps you relax. But once you are in the mountains, you may find that you do not need a book.

**What time do I need to be at the airport on the day I travel to the retreat?**
By 12:00 in the arrivals hall at Innsbruck Airport (INN) — this is when the transfer to the retreat venue leaves.

**I feel anxious before the retreat — is that normal?**
Yes. Feeling nervous before something important is a good sign. If your anxiety feels strong, message Tanya (@tatidzufri). She will offer support 🤍

**Is there anything I should do right now to prepare?**
If the retreat is more than 2 weeks away: start the online course (1 module per week) and tell the organiser about any allergies.
If there is 1 week left: pack using the packing list, let your loved ones know, and give yourself permission to slow down.
If there are 3 days left: look out for the facilitator’s questions to help you set an intention — and get some good sleep 🌿
