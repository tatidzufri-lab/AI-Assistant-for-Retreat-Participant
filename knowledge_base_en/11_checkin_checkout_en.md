## Check-in (arrival)

### Standard check-in time
- **Time:** from 14:00 to 16:00 on the first day of the programme (14 June 2026)
- Please arrive within this time window — this helps us welcome the group and start the programme

### Early check-in
Early check-in **before 14:00 is not included in the standard programme** and is only possible by prior arrangement with the organiser.

How to request early check-in:
1. Let the organiser know you would like to arrive early **at least 5 days before the start**
2. The organiser will check availability and confirm or suggest an alternative
3. Early check-in is not guaranteed

**If your flight arrives much earlier:** we recommend spending some time in the nearest town (at a café or on a walk).

### Airport transfer
The organised transfer leaves at **12:00** from Innsbruck Airport (INN), with the meeting point in the arrivals hall. If you are taking an earlier flight, please let the organiser know in advance.

### What happens at check-in
- You are welcomed and shown to your room
- You get to know the retreat space and the other participants
- You receive your workbook and materials (for the relevant packages)
- The first group meeting takes place on the evening of arrival

## Check-out (departure)

### Standard check-out time
- **Time:** by 12:00 on the last day of the programme (18 June 2026)

### Late check-out
Late check-out after 12:00 is arranged individually. It is best to request it in advance.

### Return transfer
A return airport transfer is arranged. The departure time is agreed with the group on the last day.

### What to return at check-out
- Your room key
- Art materials (paints and brushes stay at the retreat venue; your workbook and the artwork you create are yours to keep)

## Important practical details
- If unexpected circumstances affect your arrival, contact the organiser immediately (@tatidzufri)
- You will receive the organiser’s emergency contact details with your booking confirmation

## Frequently asked questions about arrival/departure

**What time do I need to be at Innsbruck Airport?**
By 12:00 on the first day (14 June). This is when the transfer leaves the airport for the retreat venue.

**Can I arrive a day before the start?**
Only by individual arrangement with the organiser. Please request this at least 5 days before the start.

**When should I leave on the last day?**
Check out of your room by 12:00 (18 June). The return airport transfer time is agreed with the group the day before.

**What if my flight home is in the evening, but check-out is at 12:00?**
Please check with the organiser in advance — it may be possible to store your luggage at the retreat venue until you leave.
