## Clothing

### For Practices and Walks
- Sports trousers or leggings (2–3 pairs)
- Loose T-shirts and long-sleeved tops (3–4 items)
- Sports tops or sports bras (2 items)
- Fleece or hoodie — **essential**: it can be cool in the mountains
- Lightweight windbreaker or jacket

### Warm Clothing
- Warm socks (3–4 pairs)
- Hat or headband for walks
- Thin gloves (if you are coming in spring or autumn)

### For Evenings and Indoors
- Light dress or comfortable outfit for evenings
- Warm indoor socks / slippers
- Pyjamas

### Footwear
- **Hiking boots or trainers with good grip** — essential for mountain walks
- Comfortable indoor shoes (slippers, flip-flops)
- Lightweight trainers for indoor practices

### What You Do NOT Need to Bring
- Evening dresses and high heels
- Business clothing
- Lots of jewellery

## Hygiene and Health

### Personal First Aid Kit
- Your regular medication (enough for your whole stay + 2 extra days)
- Pain relief (paracetamol, ibuprofen)
- Plasters, a bandage
- Antiseptic
- Allergy medication (if you are prone to allergies)
- Insect bite treatment

### Personal Care
- Toiletries
- Face and body cream
- Sunscreen **SPF 30+** (the sun is stronger in the mountains)
- Sunglasses

## For Practices
**We provide** everything you need for the sessions:
- ✅ Workbook and pens
- ✅ Art materials (paints, brushes, paper)
- ✅ Mats for meditation and practices

You do not need to buy anything specially.

## Electronics
- Phone (do not forget your charger)
- Portable charger (power bank) — in case you use your phone a lot
- Headphones — optional, for morning practices
- Camera — optional

**Reminder:** filming other participants is not allowed.

## Documents and Money
- Passport
- Health insurance policy
- A small amount of cash (for personal purchases outside the programme)
- Bank card

## What to Leave at Home
- Work laptop
- Extra jewellery
- Alcohol
- Anything that will remind you of your busy daily routine

## Frequently Asked Questions About Packing

**Do I need to bring a yoga mat?**
No. The organiser provides mats for practices — they are already at the retreat venue.

**Do I need special clothing for practices?**
No. Any comfortable sports trousers and a T-shirt will do. The main thing is that you can move comfortably.

**Are hiking boots essential?**
Yes, for mountain walks you need footwear with good grip and ankle support. Regular trainers are suitable for indoor practices, but not for the walking routes.

**What medication should I bring?**
All your regular medication + enough for 2 extra days. Pain relief, plasters and antiseptic make up a basic first aid kit. First aid is available at the retreat venue, but you need to bring your own personal medication.

**Do I need to bring bed linen and towels?**
No. The retreat venue provides clean bed linen and towels.
