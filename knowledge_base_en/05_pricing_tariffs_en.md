## Package Overview

| Package | Price | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| “Path to Self” | $390 | Online only, without the mountain retreat |
| “Rhythm of Harmony” | $1,490 | Online + 5 days in the mountains, all-inclusive |
| “Your Own Summit” | $2,290 | VIP: the most in-depth and personalised experience |

---

## “Path to Self” Package — $390

**Who it is for:** You want to begin exploring yourself at a comfortable pace at home, without attending the in-person retreat.

**What is included:**
- Access to the full online course: video modules on self-discovery
- Practical exercises (journaling, meditations)
- Access to the community support chat
- Access period: 4 weeks

**Not included:** participation in the in-person mountain retreat.

**Bonuses:**
- A personal checklist for integrating the practices into daily life
- A recording of one group meditation with the facilitator

---

## “Rhythm of Harmony” Package — $1,490

**Who it is for:** You are ready for a fully immersive experience — online preparation plus 5 days in the mountains.

**What is included:**
- The full online course (video modules, exercises, support chat)
- Participation in a 5-day in-person retreat in the Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy
- Art therapy, meditations, group sessions, walks
- A group of up to 10 people
- Accommodation in a comfortable twin room
- Three healthy meals a day
- Transfers to and from Innsbruck Airport (INN)

**Bonuses:**
- An individual 30-minute online session with a psychologist after the retreat
- An exclusive set of materials: a workbook + audio meditations for home use
- A private group chat for 3 months

---

## “Your Own Summit” Package — $2,290

**Who it is for:** You are ready for the most in-depth experience and personalised support.

**What is included (everything in “Rhythm of Harmony” plus):**
- Personalised online modules before and after the retreat
- Priority chat support (a reply within 2 hours)
- VIP in-person experience: a personal art session + a private meditation
- Accommodation in a **single room** (no roommate)
- A personal consultation with the facilitator: 1 hour before the retreat + 1 hour after
- Exclusive access to a private community for 6 months
- A gift set (eco-friendly materials for your practices)

## Frequently Asked Questions About Packages

**How much does the retreat with accommodation cost?**
The “Rhythm of Harmony” package costs $1,490 (twin room). The “Your Own Summit” package costs $2,290 (single room). Both include accommodation, meals and transfers.

**Can I attend the retreat without taking the online course?**
No. The in-person retreat always includes online preparation — they form one complete programme.

**Are instalments or split payments available?**
Please discuss instalment options directly with the organiser. The standard payment plan is a 30% deposit when booking + the remaining balance 14 days before the start.

**Are flights and transfers included?**
Transfers from Innsbruck Airport (INN) are included in the “Rhythm of Harmony” and “Your Own Summit” packages. You buy your own flight tickets.

**Can I change my package after payment?**
You can upgrade if places are available. Downgrade requests are considered individually.

**Which costs less — online or in person?**
The online-only option is the “Path to Self” package at $390. In-person participation starts at $1,490 (the “Rhythm of Harmony” package).
