## Core Principle
The retreat is a shared space. Each participant helps shape it. The rules below are not restrictions. They help create a caring atmosphere where deep personal work is possible.

## Rule 1: Confidentiality (The Las Vegas Rule)
**“What is shared in the circle stays in the circle.”**

Do not share other participants’ personal stories, feelings or disclosures outside the group. Ever. Not in conversations with friends or on social media.

## Rule 2: Respect for Others’ Feelings
- Do not give advice unless asked
- Do not judge or interpret someone else’s experience
- If something another participant says upsets you, take it as an opportunity for self-reflection

## Rule 3: Digital Detox
- Put phones away during practices and sessions
- Taking photos or videos of other participants is **strictly prohibited** without their explicit consent
- Only mention other participants in posts about the retreat with their permission
- Keep messages and calls to designated free time

## Rule 4: Be Gentle with Yourself
- You do not have to take part in any practice if you do not feel ready
- “No” is an acceptable response to any invitation from the facilitator
- If you feel unwell or are struggling, tell the facilitator so they can help

## Rule 5: Take Responsibility for Your Wellbeing
- If something causes significant discomfort, tell the facilitator rather than staying silent
- The retreat is not a place to push yourself to endure something at any cost

## Rule 6: Alcohol and Psychoactive Substances
Alcohol and any psychoactive substances are **not permitted** on the retreat premises. Their use reduces the depth of the work and the safety of the space for the whole group.

## Rule 7: Punctuality
Please arrive on time for sessions. Arriving late disrupts the group’s focus. If you are running late, let the facilitator know.

## Rules for the Support Assistant
The “Curator” assistant on Telegram follows the same principles:
- Does not discuss other participants’ personal stories
- Does not disclose business information (marketing strategies, internal metrics)
- Refers complex emotional situations directly to the facilitator, Tanya (@tatidzufri)

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Rules

**Can I take photos at the retreat?**
Yes, you can take photos of yourself and the space. But photos and videos of other participants require their explicit consent.

**Can I bring wine?**
No. Alcohol is not permitted on the retreat premises during the programme.

**Do I have to share personal experiences in group sessions?**
No. Taking part in discussions is always voluntary.

**What if another participant breaks confidentiality?**
Tell the facilitator, Tanya (@tatidzufri). This is a serious matter, and each case is handled individually.
