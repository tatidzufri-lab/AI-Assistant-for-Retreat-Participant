## What Integration Means

The retreat is not a destination, but a beginning. The first 30 days after you return are the most important: this is when insights either become part of your life or fade into everyday distractions.

Integration is not about “changing your life in a month”. It means slowly bringing your new understanding into each day, without forcing yourself. Noticing. Reconnecting with yourself. Taking your time before drawing conclusions.

Three signs that integration is going well:
- You notice your reactions in situations where you used to act automatically
- You allow yourself a pause before responding — both internally and in conversations
- You do not demand “results” from yourself — you simply observe

## Daily Practices (5–10 Minutes)

| Practice | How to Do It |
|---|---|
| **Morning meditation** | Listen to audio recordings from the retreat — before checking your phone |
| **Journal question** | Write one sentence: “What do I notice about myself today?” |

Additional journal questions:
- “What am I feeling right now — in my body and in my thoughts?”
- “What today reflected what I wanted — rather than someone else’s expectations?” |

## Guidance for the First 30 Days

| Period | What You May Experience | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | Euphoria or emptiness — both are normal | Give yourself a quiet transition; do not rush back into your routine |
| Days 7–14 | Everyday life takes over, and insights begin to fade | Open your journal and reread your retreat notes |
| Days 14–30 | Early changes or resistance | Do not force anything — notice and write things down |
| After 30 days | A new sense of what feels normal | Reflect: what has actually changed? |

## Long-Term Self-Support

After 30 days, keep going. Simple ways to support yourself:
- A monthly “check-in with yourself”: 1 hour without tasks, just your journal and the question “How am I?”
- Listen to retreat meditations when you feel unsure of your direction
- Return to your workbook when making major decisions

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What if I do not feel anything after the retreat?**
Feeling nothing is also an experience — it does not mean there were no results. Sometimes deeper changes become clear later, after a few weeks. Continue your practices without demanding immediate results from yourself.

**Do I need to tell my loved ones what happened at the retreat?**
No. The experience is yours. Share what feels right, when it feels right.

**Can I stay in touch with the group after the retreat?**
Yes. The group chat stays open — it is a space for support and meaningful connection with those who were with you in the mountains.
