## Our Approach
We do not treat or “fix” you. We create conditions where you can find your own answers. All methods aim to deepen your connection with yourself — through the body, creativity, awareness and dialogue.

## 1. Existential Analysis

**What it is:** A psychological approach that explores meaning, values, identity and freedom of choice. It is based on the work of Viktor Frankl and Alfried Längle.

**How it is used on the retreat:**
- Group sessions exploring key questions: “What matters to me?”, “What gives my life meaning?”, “What stops me from being myself?”
- Exploring inner conflicts and values
- No diagnosis or “fixing” — only exploration

**Who it is especially relevant for:** women experiencing an existential crisis, burnout or a sense of meaninglessness.

## 2. Art Therapy

**What it is:** A form of psychotherapy that uses the creative process as a tool for self-discovery.

**Important:** You do not need artistic skills. None at all. Art therapy is not about beautiful pictures. It is about the process.

**How it is used on the retreat:**
- Drawing, collage and working with images
- Each art session has a theme connected to the day’s programme
- You keep the work you create

**What it offers:** Access to feelings and experiences that are difficult to put into words. Creativity can move beyond familiar psychological defences.

## 3. Mindfulness Meditation

**What it is:** Practices for being present in the moment without judgement or analysis.

**How it is used on the retreat:**
- Morning meditations (15–30 min) — a gentle start to the day
- Evening practices — time to reflect on your experiences and slow down
- Meditation in nature (with eyes open, while moving)

**Techniques:**
- Body scan
- Breath meditation
- Open awareness meditation
- Metta meditation (loving-kindness)

**No experience is needed.** The facilitator guides you through each practice step by step.

## 4. Group Psychological Sessions

**Format:** Structured dialogue in a small group (up to 10 people).

**What happens:**
- You share what feels relevant to you (only if you wish)
- The facilitator asks questions to help you explore further
- There are no right or wrong answers
- Other participants do not give advice or make judgements — they offer attention and acceptance

**Group rule:** What is shared in the group stays in the group (the confidentiality rule).

## 5. Nature-Based Practices

**What it is:** Mindful walks and outdoor exercises.

**Types of practices:**
- Silent walks — no talking, with attention to your senses
- Meditation by the water or in the forest
- Body-based practices: feeling the ground beneath your feet, breathing the mountain air

**Physical effort:** Minimal. No difficult routes.

## What Is NOT Included in the Programme
- Hypnosis or altered states of consciousness without consent
- Forced participation or pressure
- Esoteric practices, shamanism, tarot or astrology
- Medication-based interventions
- Therapy that requires a medical licence

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Practices

**Do I need meditation experience?**
No. The facilitator guides you through each practice from the beginning. If you have never meditated before, that is completely fine.

**Do I need to know how to draw for art therapy?**
No. You do not need any artistic skills. Art therapy is about your inner process, not the quality of your drawing.

**Will personal topics be discussed in the group?**
Only if you wish. No one is required to share. The confidentiality rule applies: what is said in the group stays in the group.

**Is this psychotherapy?**
No. The retreat is for people in good psychological health and is not a substitute for psychotherapy.

**Can I choose not to take part in a practice?**
Yes. This is your space and your choice.
