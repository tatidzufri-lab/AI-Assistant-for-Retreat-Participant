## What is the Know Yourself retreat?
It is a small-group, 5-day programme in the Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy, for women who want to slow down, listen to themselves and regain inner clarity. It is not a holiday or coaching — it is deep personal work in a safe environment with like-minded women.

## Participation Format
The programme consists of two connected parts:

### Online Part (Before the Retreat)
- Video modules on self-discovery
- Practical exercises: journaling, meditation
- A support chat with participants and a facilitator
- Preparing an “intention” — a personal question or focus for the retreat

### In-Person Part (5 Days in the Mountains)
- A strict limit of 10 people in the group
- Art therapy and creative practices
- Meditations (morning and evening)
- Group psychological sessions
- Restorative nature walks
- Personal time for quiet and rest

## What You Gain

### 1. A Deep Understanding of Yourself
You leave with answers to the key questions: “Who am I?” and “What do I really want?”

### 2. Practical Tools
Not just experiences, but an action plan and specific techniques: journaling, meditations and checklists.

### 3. Community and Support
An environment of trust and understanding where you can be yourself without pretending.

### 4. Permission to Pause
Permission to stop without guilt and devote time just to yourself.

### 5. Clarity and Direction
Identify your next step and develop an inner sense of direction to guide your decisions.

## Methods We Use
- **Existential analysis** — exploring questions of meaning, identity and values (Frankl, Längle)
- **Art therapy** — expressing inner experiences creatively through images
- **Mindfulness meditations** — techniques for being present and slowing down
- **Group sessions** — working in a supportive group of women
- **Body-based practices** — walks, breathing exercises and contact with nature

## What Is NOT in the Programme
- No esoteric practices, tarot, astrology or magic
- No exhausting hikes or excursions
- No pressure to achieve results
- No “right” way to change — only your own

## Why the Dolomites?
The mountain environment is more than a beautiful setting. Nature here plays an active part in the therapeutic process:
- The quiet of the mountains helps slow your inner dialogue
- The height gives you a literal sense of perspective
- Being away from city stimulation helps you connect with yourself
- Physical contact with nature restores your connection with your body

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Concept

**Is this psychotherapy?**
No. The retreat is for people in good psychological health and is not a substitute for psychotherapy or medical care.

**Do I have to complete the online part before the retreat?**
Yes. The online preparation and in-person retreat form one programme. The online part helps you prepare mentally and emotionally for deep work in the mountains.

**What if I want to leave an activity?**
You have the right to skip any activity without explaining why. There will be no pressure.

**Will there be physically demanding activities?**
No. The walks involve minimal physical effort. There are no difficult routes. What matters is how you feel, not how far you walk.
