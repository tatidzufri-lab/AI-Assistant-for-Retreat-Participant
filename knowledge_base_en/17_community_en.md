## Participant Chat
After the retreat, everyone in the same group is invited to a private Telegram chat for:
- Support and connection with those who attended the retreat together
- Sharing insights and practices as you integrate your experience
- Questions for the support coordinator
- Updates about upcoming events

### Chat Rules
- The same as at the retreat: respect, confidentiality, and no unsolicited advice
- Advertising is not allowed in the chat
- Toxic comments are grounds for removal

## Chat Access by Package

| Package | Chat Access |
|---|---|
| “Path to Self” | Shared chat for online participants (4 weeks) |
| “Rhythm of Harmony” | Private chat for your retreat group (3 months) |
| “Your Own Summit” | Private chat for your retreat group + VIP community (6 months) |

## Support Coordinator After the Retreat

### For the “Rhythm of Harmony” Package
- Individual online session with a psychologist (30 min) 1–2 weeks after the retreat

### For the “Your Own Summit” Package
- Personal consultation with retreat facilitator Tanya (1 hour) after the retreat
- Priority responses in the chat
- Access to the private VIP community for 6 months

## Upcoming Retreats and Events
Participants who have completed the retreat receive:
- Priority booking for future retreats
- Updates about intensive online programmes and community gatherings
- Special terms for returning participants (ask the organiser for details)

## Online Resources After the Retreat
Everyone with a package that includes the in-person retreat receives:
- **Audio meditations** for independent practice at home
- **A workbook** with exercises to help integrate your experience
- **A practice checklist** for the first 30 days after the retreat

## Important Notes About the Post-Retreat Period
The first 2 weeks after the retreat are an especially sensitive time. Many participants notice:
- Heightened awareness and emotions
- A desire to change something in their lives right away
- Sometimes, temporary sadness (“post-retreat blues”)

This is normal. This is exactly why support from the coordinator and the group chat are available.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Community

**Is there a chat after the retreat?**
Yes. Everyone with an in-person package is invited to a private Telegram chat for their retreat group.

**How long is the chat available?**
“Rhythm of Harmony” — 3 months, “Your Own Summit” — 6 months + VIP community.

**Is there a consultation with a psychologist after the retreat?**
Yes. The “Rhythm of Harmony” package includes 30 min, and the “Your Own Summit” package includes 1 hour.

**Can I return for another retreat?**
Yes. Many participants return. Past participants receive priority booking.
