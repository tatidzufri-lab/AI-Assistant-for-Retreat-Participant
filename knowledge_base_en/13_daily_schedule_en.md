## Important Note
The schedule is a guide. Each group follows its own rhythm, and the facilitator adapts the programme to how participants are feeling. No two retreats are the same.

## A Typical Day (Days 2–4)

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 07:30 | Wake up gently (no alarms that disturb your roommate) |
| 08:00–08:45 | Morning practice: meditation or breathing exercises |
| 09:00–09:45 | Breakfast |
| 10:00–12:30 | Main session (art therapy or group work) |
| 12:30–13:00 | Free time |
| 13:00–14:00 | Lunch |
| 14:00–16:00 | Quiet time / free time / a walk |
| 16:00–18:00 | Practice or mindful walk |
| 18:30–19:30 | Dinner |
| 20:00–21:30 | Evening circle / reflection / informal conversation |
| 22:00 | Quiet throughout the retreat space (recommended) |

## Arrival Day (Day 1, 14 June)

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 12:00 | Meet in the arrivals hall at Innsbruck Airport (INN) |
| 12:00–14:00 | Transfer to the retreat venue (~1.5–2 hours) |
| 14:00–16:00 | Check-in and time to explore the retreat space |
| 17:00–18:30 | Group introduction and guidelines for the retreat space |
| 19:00 | First dinner together |
| 20:00–21:30 | Evening welcome circle |

## Departure Day (Day 5, 18 June)

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 07:30 | Wake up |
| 08:00–08:45 | Final morning practice |
| 09:00–09:45 | Breakfast |
| 10:00–11:30 | Closing circle and goodbyes |
| 11:30–12:00 | Packing and check-out |
| 12:00 | Departure and return transfer to the airport |

## Free Time
Every day includes **at least 2 hours** of personal time:
- A walk on your own
- Journaling and reflection
- Rest and sleep
- Informal conversation with other participants
- Simply enjoying the quiet

## Phones and Digital Detox
We recommend putting your phone away during practices and sessions. It is best to set aside a specific time to contact your family—for example, after lunch (13:00–14:00).

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Daily Routine

**What time is breakfast at the retreat?**
Around 09:00–09:45. The exact schedule is shared at the retreat.

**Is there quiet time?**
Yes. Every day from 14:00 to 16:00 is free time: you can enjoy quiet time, take a walk or rest.

**What time does the evening programme end?**
Around 21:30. From 22:00, quiet is recommended throughout the retreat space.

**Can I go to bed earlier?**
Yes. The evening circle is optional. Being gentle with yourself is the main principle.
