## Who It Is For
The online course is included in all three packages:
- **Path to Self** — the online course is the main part of this package
- **Rhythm of Harmony** and **Your Own Summit** — the course is a preparation stage before the in-person retreat

## Format
- **Platform:** private chat or member area (the link is sent after payment)
- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Recommended pace:** 1 module per week
- **Lesson format:** videos + written exercises + meditations
- **Access opens:** as soon as you pay the 30% deposit

## Course Content

### Module 1: Pause
**Topic:** How to slow down and stop acting on autopilot.
- Video: “Why We Lose Touch with Ourselves” (20 min)
- Practice: first-week journal — what do I do automatically?
- Meditation: “Here and Now” (15 min)

### Module 2: Listen
**Topic:** How to recognise your true needs and values.
- Video: “The Difference Between ‘I Have To’ and ‘I Want To’” (20 min)
- Practice: “Wheel of Values” exercise + written reflection
- Meditation: “The Voice of the Body” (15 min)

### Module 3: Understand
**Topic:** What lies behind tiredness, a loss of meaning and a personal crisis.
- Video: “Existential Crisis — a Symptom or a Resource?” (25 min)
- Practice: a letter to yourself — “What Do I Want to Change?”
- Meditation: “Acceptance” (20 min)

### Module 4: Find Direction
**Topic:** First steps towards living in harmony with yourself.
- Video: “Small Changes — Big Shifts” (20 min)
- Practice: “Road Map” — 3 intentions for the coming month
- Meditation: “Intention” (15 min)

## Support Chat

- The course mentor answers questions about the practices

**Course mentor response time:** within 24 hours on working days.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Online Course

**Do I need to complete all the exercises?**
It is recommended, but not required. The depth of your experience depends directly on your participation.

**Can I take the course at my own pace?**
Yes. The recommended pace is 1 module per week, but you can go faster or slower.

**Does the online course include video calls with the facilitator?**
Not in the Path to Self package (chat only). Packages that include the retreat also include an individual session after the retreat.

**When does access to the course open?**
As soon as you pay the 30% deposit. The organiser (@tatidzufri) sends you the link.

**What happens after 4 weeks?**
You keep access to the videos, meditations and workbook with no time limit. The support chat is available for 4 weeks from the start.

**Can I buy just the online course without the retreat?**
Yes. This is the Path to Self package. The online course is complete on its own, but does not include the in-person retreat or accommodation.

**How much time does the course take each day?**
About 30–45 minutes a day (video + practice + meditation). You can fit this around your own schedule.

**Do I need any special equipment or an app?**
No. You can access the course through a standard browser or Telegram (depending on the platform). This will be confirmed when you pay.

**What if I start the course and find it is not right for me?**
Write to the organiser (@tatidzufri) — she will review your situation individually.
