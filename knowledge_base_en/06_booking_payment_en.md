## How to Book Your Place

### Step 1. Express Your Interest
Message the organiser, Tanya (@tatidzufri), or submit the form on the website. You will receive a reply within 24 hours.

### Step 2. Get to Know Each Other
Have a short conversation with the facilitator or complete a questionnaire to make sure the retreat is right for you.

### Step 3. Choose Your Package
Choose your package ("Path to Self", "Rhythm of Harmony" or "Your Own Summit") based on your goals and budget.

### Step 4. Pay the Deposit
Your place is secured **only after you pay a 30% deposit** for your chosen package. Until then, the place remains available to other participants.

### Step 5. Confirmation
Once your deposit is received, you will receive:
- Booking confirmation
- Access to the online course (for packages that include the in-person retreat)
- A practical information pack (travel instructions, packing list)
- An invitation to the group chat

## Payment Deadlines

| Payment | Amount | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit | 30% of the package price | When booking |
| Balance | 70% of the package price | No later than 14 days before the start |

**Important:** if the balance is not received 14 days before the start, the organiser may cancel your booking.

## Payment Methods
Please check the current payment methods with the organiser when booking. The standard method is bank transfer.

## Number of Places
The group is strictly limited to **10 participants**. Places are allocated as deposits are received.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Booking

**Can I book without a deposit?**
No. Verbal agreements or messages do not secure a place. Your booking is only confirmed once your deposit has been received.

**What if the place I want is already taken?**
You can ask to join the waiting list. If someone cancels, you will be notified first.

**How can I check whether places are available?**
The latest availability is listed in the schedule file for the current retreat. For the current retreat (14–18 June 2026): 2 places in "Rhythm of Harmony" and 1 place in "Your Own Summit".

**How can I sign up right now?**
Message Tanya on Telegram: @tatidzufri — she will reply and help you choose a package 🌿
