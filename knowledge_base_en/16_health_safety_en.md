## Who the Retreat Is Suitable For

### The retreat is suitable for people in good psychological health who:
- Are going through an existential crisis but can manage everyday life
- Feel tired, burned out, or unsure of their direction
- Want to understand themselves and their values more deeply

### The retreat is NOT therapy and is not suitable for people with:
- Acute psychiatric conditions (acute psychosis, a manic episode)
- Severe depression requiring medication
- Suicidal thoughts or recent suicidal behaviour
- Active addiction (alcohol or drugs)
- A recent severe traumatic experience (less than 3 months ago), such as loss or violence

If you are unsure whether this format is right for you, contact the facilitator, Tanya (@tatidzufri), for an honest conversation.

## What to Share Before the Retreat

**You must inform the organiser 2 weeks in advance about:**
- Chronic conditions that require medication
- Allergies (food or medication)
- Dietary restrictions
- Limits on physical activity
- Pregnancy
- Psychological needs or conditions that the facilitator should be aware of

This information is strictly confidential.

## At the Retreat Venue

### First Aid Kit
A basic first aid kit is available at the venue. Bring enough of your regular medication for the full stay + 2 days.

### Emergency Medical Care
The nearest medical facilities are in the nearest town. The organiser knows the addresses and will arrange transport if needed.

### Insurance
We recommend having valid private health insurance or travel insurance.

## During Practices

### If You Feel Unwell
- Leave the practice without explaining — this is okay
- Tell the facilitator as soon as you can
- Rest and drink some water

### If You Have a Strong Emotional Reaction
Crying, feeling angry, or laughing are normal reactions to deep inner work. The facilitator is trained to support you through these experiences.

### If It Feels Like “Too Much”
Tell the facilitator. She will offer a gentler approach or a break.

## The Mountain Environment

### Acclimatisation
Even a modest altitude can cause mild dizziness or weakness during the first few hours. Drink more water and take your time.

### Weather
Mountain weather changes quickly. Bring warm clothes, even in summer. Sunscreen is essential.

### Walks
All walks must be accompanied or follow familiar routes. Only go far from the venue on your own after notifying the organiser.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Health

**Can I take part if I have anxiety?**
Yes, as long as it is not an acute condition requiring medication. If you are unsure, speak with Tanya (@tatidzufri) in advance.

**Can I attend while pregnant?**
You must inform the organiser in advance. The final decision will be made after a conversation with the facilitator.

**Do I need a medical certificate?**
It is not required. However, you will complete a questionnaire with information about your health.

**Do I need insurance?**
It is recommended, especially if you are flying from another city.

**What if I feel unwell during a practice?**
You can leave quietly at any time without explaining — this is okay and allowed.
