## Accommodation

### Room Arrangements
- You will stay in comfortable rooms at the retreat venue in the Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy
- Standard accommodation is **2 people per room**
- Single occupancy (without a roommate) is available only with the **Your Own Summit** package

### Comfort and Amenities
- Clean bed linen and towels
- Hot showers
- Heating (it can be cool in the mountains even in summer)
- Shared spaces for relaxing and spending time together

### Sharing a Room
Room assignments take compatibility and preferences into account. If you have a friend in the group and would like to share a room, please let us know in advance.

## Meals

### Meal Arrangements
Three healthy meals a day are included in the Rhythm of Harmony and Your Own Summit packages:
- **Breakfast:** ~08:00–09:00
- **Lunch:** ~13:00–14:00
- **Dinner:** ~18:30–19:30

The exact meal schedule will be announced at the venue.

### Menu
Meals are designed to support your inner work: light, nourishing dishes that do not leave you feeling heavy. The menu includes plenty of fresh vegetables, whole grains and protein.

### Dietary Requirements and Allergies
**Please make sure you tell us about any dietary requirements 2 weeks before the start!**

We can accommodate:
- Vegetarian / vegan diets
- Gluten intolerance
- Lactose intolerance
- Severe allergies (nuts, seafood, etc.)

If you tell us about your requirements later, the organiser will try to help but cannot guarantee that the menu will fully meet your needs.

### Extra Snacks
Fruit, tea and light snacks are usually available at the venue between meals. Please check when you arrive.

### Alcohol
Alcohol is not part of the programme and is discouraged at the venue during the retreat.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Accommodation and Meals

**Are single rooms available?**
Yes, but only with the Your Own Summit package ($2,290). The Rhythm of Harmony package includes twin rooms.

**I am vegetarian / I have an allergy. Can you accommodate this?**
Yes. Please make sure you tell the organiser, Tanya (@tatidzufri), 2 weeks before the start.

**Are meals included in the price?**
Yes, three meals a day are included in the Rhythm of Harmony and Your Own Summit packages. Meals are not included in the Path to Self package (online only).

**Can I bring my own food?**
Yes, especially if you have specific dietary needs. However, meals at the venue cover all your essential needs.
