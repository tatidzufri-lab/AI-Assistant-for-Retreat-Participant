## General Information
- **Brand name:** Know Yourself
- **Status:** An independently created project, launching for the first time
- **Slogan:** “Your own path. Your own rhythm. Your own summit.”
- **Location:** The Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy
- **Organiser:** Tanya (@tatidzufri on Telegram)
- **Format:** A 5-day in-person retreat + online preparation, with a group of up to 10 people

## Mission
To help you regain a sense of inner stability and clarity by creating a safe space away from daily pressures and distractions, where you can connect deeply with yourself. We believe the answers to your questions are already within you. Our role is to help you hear them through the power of nature, established methods and a supportive community.

## Idea
A modern ritual for renewal when you feel tired of multitasking, where nature acts as a therapist, silence offers guidance, and professional tools from psychology and art therapy help you understand yourself.

## Positioning
Know Yourself is a retreat in the Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy, for women experiencing an existential crisis. We create a safe, small-group setting where nature and psychological practices help you find clarity, inner stability and tools for living in harmony with yourself.

## Brand Values

### 1. Depth (Depth)
We reject superficial solutions and quick coaching formulas. We believe real transformation requires a slow, attentive and deep exploration of yourself.

### 2. Safety (Safety)
We create a trusting, small-group setting where you can feel vulnerable, weak or uncertain — however you feel — without fear of judgement. The group is limited to 10 people.

### 3. Authenticity (Authenticity)
We do not teach you to be “like everyone else” or to be “correct”. We help you hear your own voice and follow it.

### 4. Natural Harmony (Natural Harmony)
Nature plays an active part in transformation. The mountain setting, silence and natural rhythms help you slow down and reconnect with yourself.

### 5. Mindful Support (Mindful Support)
Support comes not only from the care of professionals, but also from a community of like-minded people.

### 6. Transformation through Action (Transformation through Action)
We offer not only theory, but also practical tools: journals, checklists and audio meditations.

## USPs (Unique Selling Points)
- **Small, exclusive group:** only 10 places, allowing personal attention and a trusting atmosphere
- **Nature and psychology together:** mountain silence + scientific methods (existential analysis, art therapy)
- **Lasting benefits:** tools for daily practice after the retreat

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Brand

**What is the Know Yourself retreat?**
It is a 5-day programme in the Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy, for women who want to pause, listen to themselves and regain inner clarity. It is neither a holiday nor coaching — it is deep personal work in a safe environment.

**Who organises the retreat?**
It is a project created by Tanya (@tatidzufri). You can ask her any questions directly on Telegram.

**Is it for everyone, or do I need a particular background?**
It is for you if you feel tired, have lost your sense of direction, are experiencing burnout or want a deeper understanding of yourself. No special knowledge or experience is needed.
