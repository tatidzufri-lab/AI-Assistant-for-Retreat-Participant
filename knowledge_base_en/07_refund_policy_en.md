## Basic Rules
This policy applies to the full amount paid, including both the deposit (30%) and the balance (70%), if already paid.

## Refund Table

| Time before the start | Deposit (30%) | Balance (70%), if already paid |
|---|---|---|
| **More than 30 days** | 100% refund | 100% refund |
| **14–30 days** | Non-refundable. Can be transferred to the next retreat (once) | 100% refund |
| **Less than 14 days** | Non-refundable | Non-refundable |

## Detailed Scenarios

### Scenario A: Cancellation more than 30 days before the start
- 100% of all payments will be refunded
- Refunds are processed within 10 business days using the original payment method

### Scenario B: Cancellation 14–30 days before the start
- The deposit (30%) **cannot be refunded as money**, but can be **transferred to the next retreat** (once, subject to availability)
- The balance (70%), if already paid, will be **refunded in full** within 10 business days

### Scenario C: Cancellation less than 14 days before the start
- The deposit (30%) is **non-refundable** — it covers logistics costs already paid
- The balance (70%), if already paid, is **non-refundable**
- Exceptions: force majeure circumstances supported by documentation

### Scenario D: Cancellation of the retreat by the organiser
- **100% of all payments** will be refunded, regardless of the cancellation date
- Refunds are processed within 10 business days

## Important Details

**How is the notice period calculated?**
From the date of your written cancellation notice (a message via a messaging app or email) to the first day of the retreat.

**What does transferring the deposit mean?**
Your deposit is held as an advance payment for the next available retreat. It remains valid for 12 months from the date of the original retreat.

**Can I transfer my place to someone else?**
Yes, another person can take your place with the organiser’s prior approval. The replacement participant must complete a new application form.

**Force majeure**
In documented force majeure circumstances (hospitalisation or the death of a close family member), refund requests are considered individually and with the greatest care.

## How do I cancel?
Write to the organiser, Tanya, on Telegram: @tatidzufri and include your reason for cancelling. The notice period is calculated from the moment your written message is received.
